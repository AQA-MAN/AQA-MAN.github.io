1. Purpose

To assess and evaluate, through a documented risk assessment, the potential risk to product quality, sterility assurance and classified environment (ISO 5 / Grade A) arising from the addition of a new Laminar Air Flow (LAF) unit above the filling tank in the filling area, and to define control measures to reduce identified risks to an acceptable level prior to routine use.

2. Scope

This risk assessment applies to the newly installed LAF unit positioned above the filling tank within the existing ISO 5 (Grade A) classified filling area, covering the installation, qualification (IQ/OQ/DQ/PQ) and post-installation impact on airflow pattern, differential pressure, particle count, microbial contamination control and overall room classification.

3. Responsibility

  • Quality Assurance — facilitate and approve the risk assessment; ensure change control and CAPA closure.
  • Production — provide process/operational input and ensure area readiness for installation and requalification.
  • Engineering / HVAC — provide technical input on LAF design, installation and qualification (IQ/OQ/DQ).
  • Quality Control / Microbiology — provide input on environmental monitoring requirements and results.
  • Validation — coordinate PQ / area requalification and compile qualification status.

4. Reference Documents

  • Change control — addition of new LAF above filling tank
  • IQ, OQ, DQ and PQ protocols and reports for new LAF unit
  • Site SOP for Risk Assessment / Quality Risk Management
  • Site SOP for Area Qualification / Requalification of Classified Areas
  • ISO 14644-1: Classification of Air Cleanliness
  • EU GMP Annex 1 / Schedule M — Manufacture of Sterile Products

5. Background

The filling area is an existing ISO 5 (Grade A) classified zone used for aseptic filling operations. A new LAF unit (design air flow 200 CFM, fitted with a magnehelic gauge of range 10–30 Pa for differential pressure monitoring) has been installed above the filling tank within this area to provide additional / dedicated unidirectional airflow protection at the filling tank. A change control has been raised for this modification, and IQ, OQ, DQ and PQ activities for the new LAF have been completed. This risk assessment formally evaluates the impact of this addition on the existing classified environment before the LAF is released for routine use.

7. Risk Assessment Methodology

Risk assessment is performed using the Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) approach. Each identified hazard is rated for Severity (S), Occurrence (O) and Detection (D) on a scale of 1–5.

RPN = Severity (S) × Occurrence (O) × Detection (D)

10. Summary of Residual Risk

Following implementation of the recommended control measures — including pre/post-installation smoke and airflow velocity studies, HEPA filter integrity testing, differential pressure verification, environmental monitoring and complete area requalification per ISO 14644-1 — the residual risk associated with the addition of the new LAF unit above the filling tank is reduced to the Low category for all identified hazards. No High or Medium residual risk remains open.

11. Conclusion and Recommendation

  • The addition of the new LAF unit above the filling tank does not pose an unacceptable risk to product quality or the ISO 5 / Grade A classification of the filling area, provided all recommended control measures are implemented and verified.
  • The new LAF may be released for routine production use only after successful completion of area requalification (particle count, airflow pattern/velocity, viable and non-viable environmental monitoring, differential pressure and recovery test) per approved PQ / requalification protocol.
  • All control measures shall be tracked to closure through the associated Change Control and CAPA (if applicable) prior to closure of the change control.
  • This risk assessment shall be reviewed in case of any further modification to the filling area layout, airflow design, or on recurrence of any related deviation.
